# Hook variants and packaging concepts — 2026-11-12

**Release concept:** a 60–90 s vertical slice of *The Hidden Ocean Under the Ice*.
**Evidence status:** all hooks are original writing; no competitor script was copied.

## Hook variants (min 3)

| # | First 5 seconds (spoken + visual) | Type | Why it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | "What if the ice beneath your feet could remember the temperature of the entire ocean?" — macro shot of sea ice, then a cut to a brine pocket diagram. | Question / curiosity gap | Concrete paradox; promises a mechanism, not a list. |
| H2 | "Most people think ice is just frozen water. It is not." — ice block floating in a glass, then a lattice animation. | Contradiction | Challenges a default assumption in 3 seconds. |
| H3 | "This frozen desert drives a global conveyor belt." — satellite view of polar ice, then an animated current loop. | Scale / consequence | Connects a local object to a planetary system. |

## Title options

1. **The Ice That Remembers the Ocean**
2. **Why Ice Floats — And Why It Matters**
3. **The Invisible Engine Under the Ice**
4. **What If Ice Could Remember?**
5. **The Ocean's Frozen Memory**

**Recommendation:** title 1 or 2. They name a specific object and promise a mechanism, not a generic topic.

## Thumbnail concepts (desktop + mobile)

| Concept | Composition | Text (≤4 words) | Mobile check |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Cross-section of sea ice with glowing brine channels; a small globe in the corner. | "ICE REMEMBERS" | High contrast; subject fills 60% of frame. |
| T2 | A hand holding a clear ice cube; inside the cube, a tiny ocean current loop. | "WHY IT FLOATS" | One focal point; no thin lines. |
| T3 | Split frame: frozen surface above, dark ocean below, a sinking plume of brine. | "THE HIDDEN ENGINE" | Strong top/bottom contrast. |

**Do not use:** stock footage of polar bears, generic Earth renders, or text-heavy collages.
